IEEE Paper Assistant
GitHub

Retrieval-augmented Q&A for research papers

Ask questions about any IEEE paper

Upload a paper and ask about its method, results or limitations. The assistant finds the passages that answer your question and replies with citations, so every claim can be checked against the paper.

  • Reads IEEE layouts

    Rebuilds the reading order of two-column pages and keeps figures, tables and symbols intact.

  • Hybrid search

    Combines semantic and keyword search, then reranks the best passages with a cross-encoder.

  • Cited answers

    Claude answers only from the retrieved passages and cites the ones it uses.
